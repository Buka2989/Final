About Arena ID

A student-built personality quiz inspired by the characters of Clash Royale.

How the quiz works

The questions are about everyday situations, so you can take the quiz without having played Clash Royale.

How your match is calculated

Each answer adds points to four traits: courage, strategy, teamwork, and creativity. We average those points into a profile from 0 to 100, then compare it with eight character profiles.

The character with the smallest total difference is your closest match. If two are equally close, their IDs in alphabetical order break the tie. The bars describe your answers within this quiz; they are not scientific measurements.
